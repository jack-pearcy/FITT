Support

A person reads every message.

Email support@fittapp.dev for help with the app or your account.

For questions about your data, write to privacy@fittapp.dev.

Common questions

I forgot my password

FITT does not have a self-service password reset yet. Email support from the address on your account and we will help you get back in.

How do I change my password?

In the app, open the Account tab and choose Change Password. You will need your current password. Changing it signs out every other device.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the Account tab, choose Delete Account and confirm with your password. Your account and all of your plans, sessions, sets, measurements and goals are erased. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in, email privacy@fittapp.dev from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email privacy@fittapp.dev from the address on your account and we will send it to you.

Is my data used for ads or sold?

No. FITT has no ads and shares your data with no advertiser or data broker. The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored and why.

The suggested weight looks wrong

Suggestions come from your recent logged sessions for that exercise, so a mistyped set can throw them off. Check your last sessions in the History tab and correct any set that was entered wrong. You can also change the progression style in the Account tab. One style applies to all of your exercises.

I found a bug

Email support with what you did, what you expected and what happened.

About the app
