Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version. FITT stores what you type into it so it can show it back to you, and nothing more. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your data is not sold or shared.

You can delete your account and everything tied to it from inside the iPhone app at any time. The web app has no delete screen yet, so if you use only the web app, email us and we will delete it for you.

Who this covers

This policy covers the FITT iPhone app, the FITT web app and this website, all at fittapp.dev. FITT is made and run by one independent developer, referred to here as "FITT" or "we".

What FITT stores

Every item below is something you enter yourself. FITT collects nothing in the background.

DataWhy it is stored
Email addressTo identify your account and sign you in.
PasswordStored only as a one-way hash. Nobody, including us, can read your password.
Workout plans, sessions, sets and notesThis is what the app is for: showing your training back to you and suggesting what to lift next.
Body weightTo show your trend. Only what you choose to log.
Date of birth, height, sex, activity level and goalTo work out a calorie target. Asked once during setup.
Sign-in tokensTo keep you signed in on your device. Stored on the server only as a hash.
Two-step sign-in, if you turn it onTo check the codes from your authenticator app. The authenticator secret is stored encrypted, because the server has to read it to check a code. Recovery codes are stored only as hashes.
Watch link codesTo connect your Apple Watch to your account. Each code lasts 60 seconds, works once and is stored only as a hash.

Body weight, height and the other details used for the calorie target are health-related information. FITT uses them only for the purposes in the table.

What FITT does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting kits from other companies.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No data from Apple Health.
  • No record of which screens you open or what you tap.
  • No payment details. FITT takes no payments today.

This website

The pages at fittapp.dev set no cookies and load nothing from other companies: no fonts, no scripts, no trackers. The web app keeps your sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. That token never leaves your browser except to reach FITT.

Server logs

Like any web service, the server writes technical logs when it handles a request. These can include your IP address, the time and the address requested. They are used to keep the service running and to stop abuse, such as repeated password guessing. Logs are capped in size and overwritten as they fill. They are not used to build a profile of you.

If you email us

When you write to the support or privacy address, your message and your email address are stored in a mailbox hosted by Proton. They are used only to answer you. They are not added to your FITT account or used for anything else. Ask and we will delete your messages.

The optional AI coach

A coach that writes a short review of your recent training using an AI model is coming. It is not in the app yet. It will be optional, and nothing will be sent anywhere unless you ask for a review. This section describes how it will work.

What will be sent when you ask for a review

  • The names and muscle groups of the exercises in your most recent sessions.
  • Your completed sets: weight, reps and effort rating.
  • How many days ago each session was.
  • Whether your goal is to gain, lose or hold weight.

What will never be sent

  • Your email address, your name or any account number.
  • Exact dates and times.
  • Your session notes.
  • Your body weight, target weight, date of birth, height or calorie figures.

Who receives it

The review will be written by Claude, a model made by Anthropic, PBC. The request will go from the FITT server to Anthropic. Your device will never talk to Anthropic directly.

Anthropic states that it does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models unless a customer chooses to allow that. FITT has not allowed it. Anthropic may keep request data for a limited time under its own retention rules, for example to detect misuse. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

FITT itself will not store the text it sends or the review it receives. The review will be shown to you and then gone.

Who can see your data

FITT does not sell, rent or trade your data, and does not share it for advertising. Three companies process data on FITT's behalf:

CompanyWhat it doesWhat it handles
HetznerRents FITT the server the service runs on, in the United States.Everything FITT stores sits on this server.
AnthropicRuns the AI model behind the optional coach, which is coming.Only the training summary described above, and only when you ask for a review. Nothing is sent before the coach arrives.
ProtonHosts the mailboxes behind the support and privacy addresses.Only email you choose to send us: your email address and what you write.

Two more companies provide services to FITT and receive no user data. Cloudflare runs the DNS for fittapp.dev and an uptime check that sees only whether the server answers. GitHub hosts FITT's code and runs its automated builds and checks.

We may disclose data if the law requires it. If that ever happens we will hand over only what is required.

How your data is protected

  • All connections to FITT are encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • The database is not reachable from the internet. Only the application can talk to it.
  • Repeated sign-in attempts are slowed down.

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach affecting your data is ever found, we will tell affected users without undue delay.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and every plan, session, set, note, body weight entry and goal tied to it are erased from the live database straight away. A backup of the database is taken before each update to the service and the ten most recent are kept, so a deleted account can remain in a backup until newer backups replace it. Backups are used only to restore the service after a failure.

Your choices

  • See and change your data. Your plans, workouts, body weight entries and goal are visible in the app, where you can remove them. Some things cannot be changed in the app yet: a logged set, the details you gave during setup, and your units. Email us and we will show you or correct anything FITT holds.
  • Delete your account. In the iPhone app, open the Account tab, choose Delete Account and confirm with your password. The web app has no delete screen yet, so if you use only the web app, email us and we will delete it for you.
  • Get a copy. Email us and we will send you your data in a common file format.
  • Skip the AI coach. Once the coach arrives, never ask for a review and nothing will ever be sent to Anthropic.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights, such as the right to object to processing or to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us and we will help with any request.

Children

FITT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that affects how your existing data is used will be posted here before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@fittapp.dev. For anything else: support@fittapp.dev.
