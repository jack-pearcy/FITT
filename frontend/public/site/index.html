Built around the lifting, not the feed

Everything in FITT exists to answer one question at the gym: what do I do now, and how much?

Plans that rotate

Build a plan from days and exercises. FITT keeps your place in the rotation and shows the next day when you open it.

Fast set logging

Weight, reps and how hard it felt, then Log. The fields keep your last numbers, so a repeat set is one tap, built to be used between sets with chalk on your hands.

What to lift next

A suggestion for every exercise, worked out from your recent sessions. Pick the progression style that suits you.

Body weight and goals

Log your weight, set a goal to gain, lose or hold, and get a calorie target worked out from your own numbers.

Your full history

Every finished session, with every set. Look back at a lift from last month in two taps.

An optional coach Coming

Ask for a short review of your recent training, written by an AI model. It will be off until you turn it on, and it will see your lifts, not who you are.

A quiet app for loud gyms

Dark by design, one accent colour, and the main action always in the same place.

These screens are drawn in your browser from the app's own design, with sample numbers. They are illustrations, not photographs of a device, and the Coach screen is not in the app yet.

Private by design, not by setting

Most fitness apps earn from what they learn about you. FITT has no ads and nothing to sell, so it has no reason to watch you. Every piece of data it stores is there because a screen shows it back to you.

What FITT stores

  • Your email and a scrambled password to sign you in
  • Your plans, sessions and sets the point of the app
  • Body weight and height only what you enter
  • Your goal and the numbers behind it to work out calories

What FITT never does

  • No ads, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking kits in the app
  • No selling or sharing your data
  • No location, contacts, photos or health records
  • No cookies on this website

Questions

Short answers. The long ones are in the privacy policy.

Can I download FITT today?

Not from the App Store yet. The iPhone app is in development. The web app at this address is live and uses the same account and data.

What does it cost?

Nothing today. Pricing for the iPhone app has not been decided. If FITT ever charges, it will be a plain price for the app, never payment in data.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. There are no advertisers, no data brokers and no analytics companies involved. Your data sits on a server FITT rents from Hetzner, which hosts it for FITT and nothing else. The optional coach is coming, and once it arrives the AI model behind it will see a training summary only when you ask for a review.

What exactly will the AI coach see?

The coach is not in the app yet. When it arrives it will see exercise names, your completed sets (weight, reps and effort), how many days ago each session was, and whether your goal is to gain, lose or hold. It will never receive your email, your name, any account number, exact dates, your notes, your body weight or your height. FITT will not keep the text it sends or the review that comes back.

Where is my data kept?

On one server that FITT rents from Hetzner, in the United States. Connections to it are encrypted.

How do I delete my account?

In the iPhone app, open the Account tab, choose Delete Account and confirm with your password. Your account and every plan, session, set, body weight entry and goal tied to it are erased. The web app has no delete screen yet. If you use only the web app, email privacy@fittapp.dev from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Who makes FITT?

One independent developer.
