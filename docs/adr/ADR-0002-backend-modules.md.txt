# ADR-0002: Backend Domain Modules for FITT

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2025-12-23

## Decision

For version 1, the FITT backend will be structured as a modular monolith with the following core domain modules:

- Identity
- Workouts
- Measurements
- Nutrition

Each module owns its data, rules, and persistence. Modules communicate through explicit service boundaries rather than shared, unrestricted access.

## Context

FITT is a web-first, API-first application intended to support future clients such as iOS. The backend must remain clean, understandable, and extensible while being feasible for a solo developer.

Early design decisions should avoid overly narrow domains that block future growth, while still keeping v1 focused and simple.

## Module Definitions

### Identity

Owns:
- User accounts
- Authentication and authorization
- Profile-related data (non-fitness)

Does not own:
- Fitness metrics
- Workouts
- Nutrition logic

### Workouts

Owns:
- Workout programs and routines
- Exercise selections
- Set, rep, and scheme definitions
- Progressive overload structure

Does not own:
- User identity
- Body measurements
- Nutrition calculations

### Measurements

Owns:
- Weight logs (v1)
- Historical measurement tracking
- Time-based measurement history

Future extensions may include:
- Body fat percentage
- Circumference measurements
- Performance metrics such as RPE

Does not own:
- Workout definitions
- Nutrition logic

### Nutrition

Owns:
- Calorie targets and recommendations
- Goal-based calorie logic (cut, maintain, bulk)

Does not own:
- Food databases
- Barcode scanning
- Meal-level tracking

## Dependency Rules

- Identity has no dependency on other modules
- Workouts may depend on Identity for user ownership
- Measurements may depend on Identity for user ownership
- Nutrition may depend on Identity and Measurements
- No module may directly access another module’s persistence layer

## Consequences

This structure:
- Keeps responsibilities clear
- Allows future expansion without breaking v1
- Prevents god services and cross-module coupling
- Supports long-term bodybuilding and powerlifting features such as RPE without forcing them into v1

The tradeoff is that discipline is required to maintain boundaries as the codebase grows.
